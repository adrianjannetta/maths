The calculation, step by step

1

Pressure at the boundary

pB = ρ1g h1

800 × 9.81 × 1.00 = 7.85 kPa

2

Replace it with the lower liquid

heq = (ρ1 / ρ2) h1

(800 / 1000) × 1.00 = 0.80 m

3

Measure A from the IWS

pA = ρ2g (hA + heq)

1000 × 9.81 × 2.80 = 27.47 kPa

Notice: The lower liquid is denser, so a shorter replacement column gives the same pressure. The pressure tube rises to this same level.
